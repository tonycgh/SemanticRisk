# SemanticRisk

**AI interpretation assurance for public websites.**

SemanticRisk examines what AI systems understand from an organisation’s public website: the claims they extract, where models disagree, what evidence may be shaping the result, and whether that interpretation changes over time.

> **AI visibility tells you whether you appear. SemanticRisk tells you what was understood.**

## The problem

An AI system can produce something that looks right, sounds right, and reads smoothly while still forming a materially misleading, unstable, narrowed, or misplaced interpretation.

These failures are often grouped under “hallucination,” “prompt issues,” or “content quality.” SemanticRisk treats them as a distinct interpretation-level risk.

Examples include:

- a company being placed in the wrong category;
- a broad platform being reduced to one narrow capability;
- a capability being omitted, overstated, or attributed incorrectly;
- different models extracting materially different claims from the same evidence;
- an interpretation changing even when the underlying website content appears stable.

---

## What SemanticRisk evaluates

SemanticRisk focuses on the evidence-to-interpretation chain:

1. **Capture** — what public website evidence was accessible and usable;
2. **Extract** — which claims and classifications AI systems formed;
3. **Compare** — where models agree, disagree, omit, narrow, or overstate;
4. **Monitor** — whether claims or interpretation change over time;
5. **Review** — whether the change is material and what may explain it.

This makes it possible to separate:

- content change;
- crawl or capture regression;
- model disagreement;
- interpretation drift.

## AI visibility vs interpretation assurance

| AI visibility monitoring | SemanticRisk interpretation assurance |
|---|---|
| Tracks mentions, citations, prompts, and recommendations | Records claims extracted from an organisation’s own public evidence |
| Measures whether a brand appears in generated answers | Evaluates what the model understood and how it classified the organisation |
| Supports search and marketing optimisation | Supports evidence-led review, communications, governance, and remediation |
| Monitors a prompt universe | Traces interpretation back to website wording, structure, capture, and source conditions |

SemanticRisk can sit alongside SEO, GEO, AEO, reputation, and AI-visibility platforms. It is not intended to replace them.

## Framework scope

This repository is the public reference layer for SemanticRisk. It provides language, boundaries, and concepts that can be cited, discussed, and developed independently of the commercial implementation.

SemanticRisk does **not**:

- determine objective truth;
- certify safety or compliance;
- replace human judgement;
- guarantee control over downstream AI output;
- function as a standalone decision-maker.

It is an interpretation and assurance framework, not an enforcement system.

---

## Intended uses

SemanticRisk is relevant to:

- communications and reputation teams;
- SEO, GEO, AEO, and web-strategy agencies;
- governance, assurance, and risk functions;
- evaluation of AI assistants and public-facing AI output;
- post-incident analysis;
- research, education, and policy discussion.

It is especially useful where AI-generated descriptions may influence customers, procurement teams, investors, regulators, partners, or the public.
