# SemanticRisk Public Knowledge Base Changelog

This changelog records material changes to the public SemanticRisk knowledge model.

It is not a production deployment log and does not include private implementation details.

## Unreleased

### Added

- AI-first public documentation foundation.
- Canonical SemanticRisk terminology and methodology pages.
- Product object model for domains, scans, observations, findings, reports, monitoring, and governed actions.
- AI-assistant behavioural and retrieval boundaries.
- Machine-oriented `llms.txt` discovery entry point.
- Documentation maintenance contract requiring regular review against active product development.
- Measurement-context and readiness contract for workspace domains, including free pre-scan readiness/discovery checks, versioned measurement profiles, comparison-domain portfolios, and machine-file validation boundaries.
- Dedicated Unified Report contract describing the completed report as a frozen evidence artifact with executive summary, detailed evidence sections, remediation cycle, appendices, provenance, workspace review and printable/PDF rendering.
- Free **Re-check live · No credit** contract for comparing current discovery against the evidence behind the current report without automatically spending a credit or starting a paid evidence cycle.
- Evidence-led **Review latest scan evidence** setup assistance that can compare the saved measurement profile with newer captured public claims before the next paid cycle, without consuming a credit.
- Dedicated **Compare & Monitor** contract for authorized primary/comparison-domain sets, versioned comparative measurement plans, completed comparison cycles, evidence-linked findings and the current customer workspace/report workflow.
- Compare & Monitor evidence modes for reusing current account-paid reports where available or intentionally forcing fresh evidence for every selected domain.
- Zero-credit Compare & Monitor assembly when all selected domains already have current paid evidence for the account; this builds comparison measurements/findings without launching new domain scans.

### Changed

- The public six-stage workflow is aligned to the current workspace: Access & readiness → AI visibility → Interpretation → Drift & change → Assessment & action → Unified Report. Repeat measurement is a cadence applied after the baseline.
- Measurement setup can be automatic from governed taxonomy; when a defensible category is unavailable, paid AI measurement is blocked before credit use until the market category is confirmed.
- For Compare & Monitor, the primary-company measurement context is preflighted before paid work. Supported historical context and high-confidence classification from stored public claims can resolve the primary plan; medium/low-confidence AI proposals require human review before credits are spent.
- A complete high-confidence AI classification from stored public evidence may be persisted automatically as the active primary measurement profile for comparison setup.
- Newer completed site evidence can trigger a recommendation to review the category, buyer task and decision/trust factors before the next cycle. Saving a revised profile does not itself start or fund that cycle.
- Unified Report guidance distinguishes buyer-prompt presence, owned citations, cross-page consistency, extracted-claim volume, recommendation outcomes and drift instead of treating them as one score.
- Claim count is explicitly evidence volume, not a score; a first customer-facing report is a baseline, not a trend.
- Report recommendations should prioritize measured visibility/recommendation/coverage findings ahead of optional helper-file hygiene when the evidence supports that ordering.
- A current reusable shared report may be available but locked to an account. Unlocking that current report grants access without starting another scan; another credit is offered only for an intentional fresh evidence cycle after access is granted.
- Compare & Monitor is anchored to the fixed primary company even while a competitor domain is open for inspection; competitor evidence is measured under the primary comparison context without redefining the competitor's independent profile.
- **Use current evidence where available** charges only selected domains that lack current paid evidence for the account. **Force fresh evidence for all selected domains** costs one credit per selected domain.
- When all selected comparison domains are current for the account, comparison measurement/findings can run for 0 credits. This is comparison processing over current evidence, not a free fresh domain scan.
- Detailed Compare & Monitor interpretation is presented through the primary domain's Unified Report; individual competitor reports remain supporting domain evidence.
- Weekly repeat measurement is documented as the current initial recommendation for establishing a comparison baseline, while Daily / Weekly / Monthly cadence controls remain runtime product state.
- Readiness documentation distinguishes current lightweight live discovery from older **Last full site evidence** and preserves the current report's full evidence as the preferred re-check comparison baseline.
- A material readiness/discovery change may justify recommending a fresh 1-credit evidence cycle, but the free live re-check itself consumes no credit and does not automatically start the paid cycle.
- AI runtime-state guidance includes readiness result, live re-check result, measurement-profile freshness/version, report grant, portfolio membership, comparison-set membership, selected domains, evidence mode, comparison credit cost, comparison-plan version, comparison-cycle state, cadence execution and work state.
- Compare & Monitor core measurements are documented as AI-market outcomes rather than a feature checklist. Direct AI answer outcomes are captured separately from later web-grounded factual verification and citations.
- Comparison findings are presented as **Do this**, **Watch this**, and **Advantage**, remain linked to persisted measurement evidence, and must use evidence-bounded language rather than treating "not observed" as proof of competitor absence.
- Compare & Monitor does not create an unexplained composite winner/rank score. Recognition, recommendation, citation, interpretation, governance and drift remain separable evidence dimensions.
- The former US$200 Comprehensive AI Visibility & Interpretation Review is historical and is not a current commercial offer. Current pricing and credit rules are governed by the live SemanticRisk product sources.

### Historical note

Earlier changelog entries described the US$200 Comprehensive Review as current at the time it was introduced. That offer has since been retired. Historical documentation may remain for provenance but must not override current pricing or product definitions.

### Maintenance policy

During active development, documentation should be reviewed against production changes at least once per development day. Material customer-facing behaviour changes should update the relevant documentation before being considered documentation-complete.
