# Compare & Monitor

Compare & Monitor is the current recurring comparative product for one fixed primary domain and up to three selected competitors.

Current public price: **US$199/month or US$1,990/year**.

It is designed to answer: **where is the primary organisation being surfaced, displaced, misunderstood, weakly supported or advantaged relative to selected competitors, and what should be done or watched next?**

## Fixed primary context

The comparison set contains one fixed primary domain plus the selected comparison domains. Opening or inspecting a competitor does not make it the comparison primary.

The primary company's governed measurement context anchors the comparison so the same buyer/category frame is applied across the selected set.

Competitor domains remain independent evidence objects with their own site evidence and reports. A comparison relationship does not imply ownership, control or affiliation.

## Current measurement breadth

The current Compare & Monitor commercial cycle uses **40 neutral buyer/research questions** from Buyer Visibility Matrix v1 under the fixed primary-company context.

Questions cover stable decision dimensions and research perspectives such as discovery, category association, buyer fit, recommendation/shortlist outcomes, evidence/citation behaviour, trust/risk and comparative interpretation. Questions must not force evaluated vendors into answers merely to create positive visibility.

Exact provider/model lanes, repetitions and observation totals are versioned runtime state.

## Evidence reuse

Domain evidence acquisition and comparison computation are separate concerns.

Where the active subscription and evidence-compatibility rules permit it, SemanticRisk can reuse compatible current evidence instead of needlessly repeating the same site/provider work. This reuse is an internal entitlement/evidence decision; customers are not asked to manage the retired per-domain credit model.

If fresh evidence is required, the workspace should show truthful queued/running/finalizing state rather than implying that an older report is the new cycle.

## Findings

Comparison findings remain linked to persisted evidence. Current customer-facing finding types include:

- **Do this** — evidence-backed action or investigation;
- **Watch this** — condition worth re-measuring or reviewing;
- **Advantage** — favourable relative outcome supported by the measured evidence.

A finding may interpret evidence but must not manufacture an unexplained composite winner score or turn "not observed" into proof that a competitor lacks a capability.

## Direct answer and grounded verification

For configured core AI-market measurements, SemanticRisk can record the direct AI answer surface first and keep later web-grounded factual verification separate.

A supporting URL collected during verification does not mean the original direct/no-web answer cited that URL. Direct measured outcome and later support evidence remain distinct observation layers.

## Report destination

Detailed comparison interpretation belongs in the **primary domain's Unified Report**. Competitor reports remain supporting domain evidence.

A completed comparison can include recognition, category association, recommendation/shortlist outcomes, citations/source support, interpretation/grounding gaps, competitor displacement, governance/risk-sensitive outcomes and change over time.

## Runtime-state boundary

Documentation does not establish a customer's current selected competitors, evidence freshness, active plan, current comparison cycle or report readiness.

Use authenticated runtime state for those facts. Simply viewing comparison state must not start another measurement cycle.
