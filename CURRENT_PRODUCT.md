# SemanticRisk Current Product

Current as of 2026-09-10.

SemanticRisk provides AI visibility, interpretation accuracy and semantic drift intelligence for public websites through one six-stage workflow: Access & readiness, AI visibility, Interpretation, Drift & change, Assessment & action, and Unified Report. Repeat measurement is a cadence applied after the baseline.

Any public domain can be measured without proving ownership. One SemanticRisk credit costs US$5.95. Standard packs are 1, 10, 20 and 100 credits. One credit grants a current reusable Unified Report when no more than 30 days old, or funds/joins the next shared public-domain evidence cycle when no fresh reusable report exists. An explicit early refresh consumes another credit.

## Evidence-cycle integrity and repair

A fresh paid evidence cycle must produce a real representative site-evidence set before controlled AI measurement can begin. Fresh discovery that produces zero eligible representative pages is blocked rather than treated as a successful site-evidence phase, so SemanticRisk does not publish an empty coherent Unified Report.

A SemanticRisk-side failure that produced a structurally invalid paid coherent report does not require the customer to buy another credit merely to repair that failed product outcome. A replacement repair cycle can reuse the entitlement from the previously funded invalid report while preserving that invalid artifact for audit history.

Paid cycles also ensure semantic synthesis from the same bound site audit when governed claims are available. This synthesis is part of the funded evidence/report workflow and does not consume an additional customer credit.

Structurally invalid coherent-cycle artifacts are excluded from normal customer trend/history semantics. Drift is compared only against valid comparable evidence. If an exact stored delta against the previous valid report exists, that delta can be shown; otherwise SemanticRisk abstains with **Comparable drift unavailable** instead of manufacturing a replacement drift direction.

## Compare & Monitor

Compare & Monitor anchors a selected competitor set to the customer's fixed primary company and its governed AI Measurement Plan.

Customers can choose **Use current evidence where available**, which reuses still-current reports already paid for by that account and charges only selected domains needing fresh evidence, or **Force fresh evidence for all selected domains**, which requests one fresh evidence cycle per selected domain.

When every selected domain already has current paid evidence for the account, SemanticRisk can build the comparison cycle and findings from those current reports for **0 credits** and without launching new domain scans. Comparison processing still runs because the comparison measurement and findings must be produced.

The zero-credit current-evidence path performs a synchronous preflight before comparison processing begins. It resolves the primary measurement context, validates active competitor membership and verifies that an active comparison plan contains runnable measurements. A missing or empty active plan can be rebuilt from current stored evidence without a domain rescan or credit charge. If preflight cannot prepare a runnable comparison, the workspace exposes the actual failure reason rather than presenting indefinite processing.

If comparison observations complete but final findings fail, retry reuses the completed observations and regenerates only the findings at **0 credits**. A missing current cycle or a completed cycle from an older incompatible probe is also presented as retryable; retry builds the current comparison measurement from the selected current evidence without rescanning the domains or charging another credit.

When comparison findings are complete, **View Compare & Monitor Report** opens the fixed primary company's current Unified Report directly. Clicking the completed state does not start another comparison cycle. Competitor reports remain supporting domain evidence.

## Measurement context

Comparison work is preflighted against one primary-company AI Measurement Plan before the comparison measurement starts, including the zero-credit current-evidence path. Supported existing context is preferred. Where stored public claim evidence supports a complete high-confidence classification, the current product can persist an active primary measurement profile automatically; lower-confidence proposals require human review before paid work proceeds.

## Report source and provenance boundary

For configured direct-answer comparison measurements, the direct/no-web answer remains the measured outcome. A later grounded verification pass can persist public source URLs as supporting provenance without implying that the original direct answer cited those URLs. Stored source URLs and direct-answer citations must therefore not be treated as interchangeable.

The Unified Report may normalize customer-facing wording and layout at render time without rewriting its frozen evidence. A reader guide, competitive scorecard and explanatory labels are presentation aids; they do not change measurement outcomes, scores, prompts, or frozen report evidence.
