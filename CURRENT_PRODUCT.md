# SemanticRisk Current Product

Current as of 2026-09-08.

SemanticRisk provides AI visibility, interpretation accuracy and semantic drift intelligence for public websites through one six-stage workflow: Access & readiness, AI visibility, Interpretation, Drift & change, Assessment & action, and Unified Report. Repeat measurement is a cadence applied after the baseline.

Any public domain can be measured without proving ownership. One SemanticRisk credit costs US$5.95. Standard packs are 1, 10, 20 and 100 credits. One credit grants a current reusable Unified Report when no more than 30 days old, or funds/joins the next shared public-domain evidence cycle when no fresh reusable report exists. An explicit early refresh consumes another credit.

## Compare & Monitor

Compare & Monitor anchors a selected competitor set to the customer's fixed primary company and its governed AI Measurement Plan.

Customers can choose **Use current evidence where available**, which reuses still-current reports already paid for by that account and charges only selected domains needing fresh evidence, or **Force fresh evidence for all selected domains**, which requests one fresh evidence cycle per selected domain.

When every selected domain already has current paid evidence for the account, SemanticRisk can build the comparison cycle and findings from those current reports for **0 credits** and without launching new domain scans. Comparison processing still runs because the comparison evidence and findings must be produced.

Detailed Compare & Monitor interpretation is presented through the primary domain's Unified Report. Competitor reports remain supporting domain evidence.

## Measurement context

Paid comparison work is preflighted against one primary-company AI Measurement Plan before credits are used. Supported existing context is preferred. Where stored public claim evidence supports a complete high-confidence classification, the current product can persist an active primary measurement profile automatically; lower-confidence proposals require human review before paid work proceeds.
