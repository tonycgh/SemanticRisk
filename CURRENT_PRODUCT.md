# SemanticRisk Current Product

Current as of **2026-09-13**.

SemanticRisk provides AI visibility, interpretation, comparison and semantic-drift intelligence for public websites through one customer workspace and one Unified Report.

## Commercial model

The current public product ladder is:

- **Free Public Check — $0**: anonymous public-domain diagnostic.
- **Comprehensive Review — $200 one time**: broad evidence cycle and Unified Report.
- **Monitor — $99/month or $990/year**: recurring measurement and reporting for one domain.
- **Compare & Monitor — $199/month or $1,990/year**: recurring primary-domain measurement plus comparison against up to three selected competitors.

The former $5.95 credit-pack product is retired from the public commercial model. Historical credit, payment and grant records can remain for compatibility and audit integrity, but new customers should not be offered credit packs.

## Evidence-cycle sequence

A current paid evidence cycle binds the following to one coherent report generation:

1. public discovery and representative site evidence;
2. semantic synthesis and evidence-grounded measurement context;
3. controlled buyer/research AI measurement;
4. interpretation and source/provenance evidence;
5. comparison and drift evidence where compatible prior or competitor evidence exists;
6. the frozen Unified Report artifact.

Fresh discovery must produce usable representative evidence before downstream AI measurement proceeds. A system-caused structurally invalid report should be repaired under the existing Review or subscription entitlement rather than requiring the customer to purchase the same outcome again.

Already-entitled work can continue server-side without the customer keeping the workspace open.

## Measurement context

Before controlled AI measurement begins, SemanticRisk derives or confirms a versioned context from current evidence. It can include organisation/entity, broad sector, buyer-facing category, buyer decision need, decision/risk need and geography where relevant.

High-confidence SemanticRisk-owned context can be versioned automatically from current evidence. Customer-confirmed category context and genuinely custom buyer/risk wording should not be silently overwritten. Broad terms such as `global`, `worldwide` and `international` are treated as unbounded geography rather than literal prompt locations.

## Current buyer/research question matrix

The current commercial matrix is **Buyer Visibility Matrix v1**, built as ten decision dimensions across six stable research perspectives.

- Comprehensive Review: **60** neutral buyer/research questions.
- Monitor: **30** questions per recurring commercial cycle.
- Compare & Monitor: **40** questions using the fixed primary-company context across the selected comparison set.

Questions are evidence-grounded and must not disclose or force the target brand into discovery/recommendation prompts merely to create a positive result.

Exact provider/model lanes, repetitions and required observation totals are calculated from the active measurement plan at runtime. Historical six-question batteries remain valid historical evidence but are not the current commercial breadth.

## Compare & Monitor

Compare & Monitor anchors comparison to one fixed primary company and its governed measurement context. Competitor domains remain independently measurable evidence objects, but comparison questions use the primary-company buyer/category frame so results are comparable.

The plan supports the primary domain plus up to three selected competitors. Compatible current evidence can be reused internally when the active entitlement permits it; customers are not asked to manage per-domain credit purchases in the current commercial model.

Detailed comparison interpretation belongs in the primary domain's Unified Report. Competitor reports remain supporting evidence rather than separate competing product surfaces.

## Unified Report

The Unified Report is a frozen evidence artifact for a completed cycle. Depending on available evidence it can contain readiness, website evidence, controlled AI observations, direct representation, grounded verification, citation/source provenance, representation/grounding gaps, claim assessment, competitor displacement, drift/change, evidence-linked actions, advantages, monitoring priorities and appendices.

A first valid cycle establishes a baseline. Drift requires compatible repeat evidence. Structurally invalid artifacts are not normal trend baselines, and where no exact valid comparison exists the correct state is **Comparable drift unavailable**.

## Runtime authority

The live SemanticRisk product is authoritative for current entitlement, work state, evidence freshness, report availability, monitoring execution, comparison state and action availability.
